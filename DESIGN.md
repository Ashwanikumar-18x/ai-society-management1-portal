# Visual Design Contract

- Product mode: `operate`
- Audience and cadence: Residents tracking household & community complaints; Society operations managers triaging tickets in real-time.
- Visual world: Modern Civic Operations — clean slate-50 backgrounds, pure white elevation cards with color-coded left-accent border indicators, vibrant cobalt blue (#2563EB) primary actions, soft pastel badge pills, and deep navy (#081028) split-screen authentication.
- Palette family + neutral undertone: Slate neutral undertone, primary cobalt blue (#2563EB), emergency rose (#EF4444), warning amber (#F59E0B), success emerald (#10B981).
- Type treatment: Inter Variable with distinct uppercase overlines (e.g. `RESIDENT DASHBOARD`, `CIVIC OPERATIONS CENTER`, `AI-PRIORITIZED WORK QUEUE`), bold numerical stats, font-mono identifiers (`#1ADF54`).
- Composition: 2-column operational dashboard with 4/5-metric stat rows, fast filter toolbars, and inspection detail panels.
- Shape language: `rounded-2xl` for primary containers, `rounded-xl` for buttons and inputs, `rounded-full` for status pills.
- Anti-references: No greeting text ("Good Morning", "Good Evening"); no generic gray SaaS; no dead links.
